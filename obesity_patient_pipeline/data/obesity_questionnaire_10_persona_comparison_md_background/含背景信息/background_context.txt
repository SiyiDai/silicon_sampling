# Survo competitor

# **Desk research (pre-survey)**

## KTAs

- Mazdutide and Survodutide targets both appetite suppression plus increased energy expenditure --> this is different in terms of being more comprehensive than others

    - First generation GLP-1 like Semaglutide focuses on appetite suppression; future medications such as Orforglipron are also appetite suppression only, but more convenient due to oral pills instead of injection

    - Tirzepatide targets metabolic control through incretin hormones

- Between Mazdutide and Survodutide, patient motivations may differ: Survo --> metabolic health (fatty liver) vs Maz (appearance)

## **Overview, by provider**

## **Medical investment, by mechanism**

GLP-1 (Glucagon-Like Peptide-1, in all): GLP-1 is a natural gut hormone released after eating. It signals to the brain and pancreas that food has been consumed and helps regulate metabolism.

- reduces appetite: acts on appetite centers in the hypothalamus in the brain --> increases satiety (feeling full), reduces hunger signals

- slows gastric emptying: slows the rate at which food leaves the stomach, slower absorption of glucose

- improves insulin secretion: stimulates insulin release from pancreatic beta cells, but only when blood glucose is elevated

GIP (Glucose-Dependent Insulinotropic Polypeptide, in Tirzepatide): another incretin hormone released by the gut after eating; it mainly regulates glucose metabolism and insulin sensitivity.

- improves insulin sensitivity: helps the body’s cells respond better to insulin, by muscle and fat cells absorb glucose more efficiently + blood glucose levels decrease

- enhances glucose metabolism: enhances insulin secretion when glucose levels rise

- works strongly for diabetes control: supports pancreatic beta cells --> helps maintain long-term glucose regulation

Glucagon (in Mazdutide / Survodutide): known as a hormone that raises blood glucose, but when used therapeutically in combination with GLP-1, it has important metabolic effects on energy expenditure and fat metabolism

- increases energy expenditure: stimulates metabolic activity in the liver and other tissues --> body burns more energy even at rest

- stimulates fat metabolism: promotes lipolysis, the breakdown of stored fat

- reduces liver fat: improves liver metabolism

# **Research second stage - Based on survey (in Chinese)**

## 利拉鲁肽（诺和力、利鲁平）

### 药物类别

GLP-1（胰高糖素样肽-1）受体激动剂

### 作用机制

利拉鲁肽通过模拟人体天然 GLP-1 激素发挥作用：

- 作用于大脑食欲中枢，增强饱腹感，减少食欲

- 延缓胃排空，减缓食物吸收速度

- 在血糖升高时促进胰岛素分泌

- 降低餐后血糖波动

### 主要特点

- 属于较早上市的长效 GLP-1 类药物

- 临床应用经验丰富

- 同时具备降糖与减重效果

- 需每日注射一次

### 适应症方向

- 2 型糖尿病

- 超重及肥胖管理

- 2 型糖尿病
